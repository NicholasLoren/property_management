## Overview

A unit is a single rentable space: a whole standalone house, or one
apartment/room/shop inside a multi-unit property.

## Viewing units

**Units** in the sidebar shows every unit across your entire portfolio, with
its property, status (vacant/occupied), price, and feature count.

![The all-units list across every property, with status, price, and feature columns](/docs-screenshots/units/units-01-all-index.png)

To see just one property's units, open that property and use its **Units**
tab, or go directly to **Properties → (a property) → Units**.

![A single property's Units tab](/docs-screenshots/units/units-02-property-index.png)

## Adding a unit

From a property's Units tab, click **Add unit**.

![The empty Add unit form: Name, Unit type, Size, Status, Price, and Notes](/docs-screenshots/units/units-03-create-empty.png)

Fill in a name, pick a **Unit type** (Studio, Single room, Apartment, and so
on, configured in [Extras](/docs/extras/overview)), size, status, and
price. **Notes** are internal-only and never shown to tenants.

![The form filled in with a name, size, price, and notes](/docs-screenshots/units/units-04-create-filled.png)

Click **Add unit**.

## Viewing a unit

A unit's page shows its details, price history, and tabs for its **current
tenant**, **past tenants**, **payments**, **leases**, **performance**, and
**maintenance** requests.

![A unit's detail page, showing details, price history, and a vacant Current tenant tab](/docs-screenshots/units/units-05-show.png)

## Editing a unit

Click **Edit** from the unit's page, or the pencil icon from any units list.
Changing the price here starts a new entry in **Price history** rather than
overwriting the old one, so past rent amounts stay on record.

![The edit unit form, pre-filled with the unit's current details](/docs-screenshots/units/units-06-edit.png)

## Removing a unit

The trash icon on any units list moves a unit to the trash, the same
reversible pattern as [Properties](/docs/properties/overview): a toast with
**Undo** appears immediately, and it can be permanently deleted later from
the Trash tab.
