## Overview

Notifications are system-generated alerts addressed to you: rent
reminders and similar automated notices. You don't create these yourself;
they show up as events in the system warrant them.

## Checking notifications

Click the bell icon in the top bar for a quick preview of your most recent
notifications.

![The notification bell dropdown, open, showing "No notifications yet"](/docs-screenshots/notifications/notifications-02-bell-dropdown.png)

Click **See all notifications**, or **Notifications** in the sidebar, for
the full list with search.

![The full Notifications page, empty](/docs-screenshots/notifications/notifications-01-index.png)

## Marking as read

Clicking a notification marks it read and takes you to whatever it's
about. **Mark all as read** (shown whenever you have unread notifications)
clears your unread count in one action.
