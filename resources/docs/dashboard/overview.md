## Overview

The Dashboard is what you land on after signing in. It's a snapshot of your
whole portfolio for the current year: headline numbers at the top, then
charts and a per-property breakdown below.

![The Dashboard, showing summary cards for net profit, income, expenses, and arrears, plus collection rate, income vs expenses, and expenses-by-category charts](/docs-screenshots/dashboard/dashboard-01-overview.png)

## What's on it

- **Summary cards**: net profit, income, expenses, and arrears for the
  current year, in your organization's configured currency.
- **All Properties filter**: narrow every number and chart on the page down
  to a single property; **Reset** clears the filter.
- **Collection rate**: the percentage of billed rent actually collected,
  month by month.
- **Income vs expenses**: a combined chart of both over recent months;
  hover a point for the exact figures.
- **Expenses by category**: a breakdown of where spending is going.
- **By Property**: scroll down for a per-property table of the same key
  numbers.

Everything on this page is read-only; it's a summary, not a place to make
changes. Use the sidebar to get to the module you need (Properties, Leases,
Payments, and so on).
