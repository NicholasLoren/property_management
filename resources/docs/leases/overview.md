## Overview

A lease ties a unit to one or more tenants for a fixed term, with rent
terms, a generated agreement document, and e-signatures.

## Viewing leases

**Leases** lists every tenancy agreement, its term, rent, and status
(**Draft**, active, ended, and so on).

![The Leases list, showing unit, tenants, term, rent, and status](/docs-screenshots/leases/leases-01-index.png)

## Adding a lease

Click **Add lease**.

![The empty Add lease form: Unit, Status, Start/End date, Rent, Billing period, Rent due day, Security deposit, Tenants, Notes, and a lease document upload](/docs-screenshots/leases/leases-02-create-empty.png)

Search for and select the **Unit**:

![The Unit dropdown filtered to "Unit 2B", with one matching result](/docs-screenshots/leases/leases-03-create-unit-search.png)

Then fill in the term (start and end dates are both required), rent amount
and billing period, the day of the month rent is due, an optional security
deposit, and add one or more **Tenants** by searching and selecting each
one. You can optionally attach an existing signed lease document instead of
generating one.

![The form filled in with a unit, dates, rent, and one tenant added as a badge](/docs-screenshots/leases/leases-04-create-filled.png)

Click **Add lease**. You're returned to the list; find your new lease
there.

## Viewing a lease

A lease's page shows its term, rent, tenants, a **Signatures** section, and
tabs for payment history and the upcoming payment schedule. **Download
agreement** generates a PDF of the lease terms at any time.

![A lease's detail page, showing rent/term summary, tenants, and an unsigned Signatures section for the landlord and tenant](/docs-screenshots/leases/leases-05-show.png)

## Collecting signatures

Each party (the landlord and every tenant) has their own **Sign now**
button, for witnessed in-person signing on the same device, or **Send link**
(tenants only, when they have an email or phone on file) to let them sign
remotely on their own device; see
[what a tenant sees when signing remotely](/docs/leases/tenant-signing).

Click **Sign now** to open the signing dialog: confirm the signer's name,
then sign in the box using a mouse, stylus, or finger.

![The "Sign as Landlord" dialog, with a signer name field and a blank signature box](/docs-screenshots/leases/leases-06-sign-dialog.png)

![The same dialog with a signature drawn in the box](/docs-screenshots/leases/leases-07-sign-drawn.png)

Click **Save signature**. That party's row updates immediately to show it's
been signed, with the date.

![The lease page after signing, showing "Signed [date]" for the landlord while the tenant is still unsigned](/docs-screenshots/leases/leases-08-signed.png)

A lease is fully executed once every party listed has signed.
