## Overview

When you click **Send link** on a lease (see
[Adding, signing, and managing leases](/docs/leases/overview)), the tenant
gets a one-time link by email or SMS. It opens a public page — no login
needed — matching the app's own branding.

## What the tenant sees

The lease's unit, term, and rent are shown for review, along with a field
to sign in (pre-filled with their name) and a signature box.

![The tenant-facing signing page, showing the lease summary, a "Signing as" name field, and a blank signature box](/docs-screenshots/signing/signing-01-empty.png)

They sign with a mouse, stylus, or finger, then click **Sign agreement**.

## After signing

The page confirms the signature was recorded. The link is single-use — if
someone opens it again afterward (or it's opened a second time by
mistake), they see the same confirmation instead of being able to sign
again.

![The signing page after completion, showing "Already signed. This tenancy agreement has already been signed. Thank you!"](/docs-screenshots/signing/signing-03-signed.png)

Back on the lease's page, that tenant's row in **Signatures** updates to
show they've signed, the same as if you'd recorded it in person with
**Sign now**.
