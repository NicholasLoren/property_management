## Overview

Payments record rent actually received against a lease's scheduled due
dates. Each lease automatically generates a schedule of rent periods when
it's created; payments are recorded against those periods, not entered as
free-standing transactions.

## Viewing payments

**Payments** lists every payment recorded, with the total collected shown
at the top.

![The Payments list, showing tenant, amount, date, method, and status, with a total collected figure](/docs-screenshots/payments/payments-01-index.png)

## Recording a payment

Click **Record payment** and select the **Lease** it's for.

![The empty Record payment form, with only a Lease field visible until one is selected](/docs-screenshots/payments/payments-02-create-empty.png)

Once a lease is selected, its outstanding **rent period(s)** appear as
checkboxes, each showing the amount due and its current status (Pending,
Partial, and so on).

![The rent period checkboxes for the selected lease, listing several upcoming and overdue periods with amounts due](/docs-screenshots/payments/payments-03-create-periods.png)

Check one or more periods; the **Amount** fills in automatically as their
total, though you can adjust it (useful for partial payments). Set the
payment date, method, and status, and optionally a reference number and a
scanned receipt.

![The form with a rent period checked, the amount auto-filled, and a payment date set](/docs-screenshots/payments/payments-04-create-filled.png)

Click **Record payment**.

![The payments list after recording one, showing an updated total collected and a confirmation](/docs-screenshots/payments/payments-05-recorded.png)

## Receipts

Every recorded payment generates a numbered receipt automatically;
tenants and landlords can find it from the payment's entry in a lease's or
tenant's payment history.

## Editing or removing a payment

Click the pencil icon on any payment's row to correct its details. The
trash icon moves it to the trash, reversible via **Undo** immediately after
or from the Trash tab later.
