## Overview

If you've forgotten your password, you can reset it yourself from the login
page — no need to ask an administrator.

## Step 1: Request a reset link

On the login page, click **Forgot password?** next to the password field.
You'll land on the forgot-password screen.

![The forgot password page with a single email field and an "Email password reset link" button](/docs-screenshots/authentication/forgot-01-empty.png)

Enter the email address on your account.

![The forgot password form filled in with an email address](/docs-screenshots/authentication/forgot-02-filled.png)

Click **Email password reset link**. Whether or not that email exists in the
system, you'll see the same confirmation — this avoids revealing which
emails are registered.

![The forgot password page after submitting, showing the message "We have emailed your password reset link."](/docs-screenshots/authentication/forgot-03-sent.png)

## Step 2: Open the emailed link

Check your inbox for a password reset email and click the link in it. It
opens the reset-password page with your email address already filled in
(read-only) and blank password fields.

![The reset password page with the email pre-filled and empty password fields](/docs-screenshots/authentication/reset-01-empty.png)

## Step 3: Choose a new password

Enter and confirm your new password.

![The reset password form filled in with a new password and matching confirmation](/docs-screenshots/authentication/reset-02-filled.png)

Click **Reset password**. You're taken back to the login page with a
confirmation, and you can sign in with your new password right away.

![The login page after a successful reset, showing the message "Your password has been reset."](/docs-screenshots/authentication/reset-03-success.png)

Reset links expire after a while and can only be used once — if yours has
expired, just request a new one from the login page.
