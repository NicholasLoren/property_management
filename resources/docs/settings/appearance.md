## Overview

**Account settings → Appearance** controls light/dark theme for your own
browser — it's a per-device preference, not shared with anyone else on your
team.

## Choosing a theme

Pick **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** (matches your operating system's
current setting). The change applies immediately across the whole app.
