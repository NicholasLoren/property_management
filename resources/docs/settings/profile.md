## Overview

**Account settings → Profile** is where you update your own name and email
address. Get there from the user menu in the bottom-left of the sidebar, or
directly at `/settings/profile`.

## Step 1: Edit your details

The form is pre-filled with your current name and email.

![The Profile settings page with Name and Email fields, and a Save button](/docs-screenshots/settings/profile-01-current.png)

Change either field and click **Save**.

## Step 2: Confirmation

A "Profile updated." confirmation briefly appears in the bottom-right corner.

![The Profile settings page after saving, with a "Profile updated." toast notification](/docs-screenshots/settings/profile-02-saved.png)

## Deleting your account

Further down the same page is a **Delete account** section. This is
permanent and removes all of your resources — you'll be asked to enter your
password to confirm before anything is deleted.
