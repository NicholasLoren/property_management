## Overview

Tenants are the people (or companies) who rent from you. A tenant exists
independently of any lease — you add them once, then attach them to one or
more leases over time.

## Viewing tenants

**Tenants** lists everyone, with search and filters.

![The Tenants list, showing tenant name, contact info, and lease status](/docs-screenshots/tenants/tenants-01-index.png)

## Adding a tenant

Click **Add tenant**.

![The empty Add tenant form: Avatar, Tenant type, Name, Email, Phone, ID number, Address, and Notes](/docs-screenshots/tenants/tenants-02-create-empty.png)

Only **Name** is required — everything else can be filled in later. Toggle
**Tenant type** to **Company** to register a business tenant instead of a
person; this swaps in a company name, registration number, and (if your
organization has compliance tracking turned on) TIN and a contact person.

![The form with "Company" selected, showing company name, registration number, compliance fields (TIN, ID document type), and a contact person section](/docs-screenshots/tenants/tenants-03-create-company.png)

For an individual tenant, fill in name, email, phone, ID number, and
address as needed.

![The form filled in as an individual tenant, with name, email, ID number, and address](/docs-screenshots/tenants/tenants-04-create-filled.png)

Click **Add tenant**. You're returned to the tenants list with a
confirmation.

## Viewing a tenant

A tenant's page shows their active leases, total paid, outstanding balance,
profile and compliance details, and tabs for **Payments**, **Upcoming**
charges, and **Documents**.

![A tenant's detail page, showing stats, profile & compliance info, and a Payments tab with no payments yet](/docs-screenshots/tenants/tenants-05-show.png)

A tenant with no leases yet, like a newly added one, shows "No leases yet"
— attach them to one from [Leases](/docs/leases/overview).

## Editing a tenant

Click **Edit** from the tenant's page or the pencil icon on any tenants
list — the same form as creating one, pre-filled.

![The edit tenant form, pre-filled with the tenant's current details](/docs-screenshots/tenants/tenants-06-edit.png)

## Removing a tenant

The trash icon moves a tenant to the trash, the same reversible pattern
used throughout the app — an **Undo** option appears immediately, and
permanent deletion is only available from the Trash tab afterwards.
