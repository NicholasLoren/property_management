## Overview

**Activity log** (Admin section of the sidebar) is a permanent audit trail
of every action taken in the app: logins, and every record created,
updated, or deleted. It can't be edited or removed by anyone.

![The Activity log, listing events with their category, who did it, device, IP address, and when](/docs-screenshots/logs/logs-01-index.png)

## Reading an entry

Each row shows a plain-language description of what happened, a category
badge (auth, user, transaction, payment, document, message, and so on),
who did it, what device and browser, their IP address, and when. Click a
row for the full details.

![A log entry's details panel, showing the event, category, actor, device, and IP address](/docs-screenshots/logs/logs-02-details.png)

Use the search box to find activity by keyword: a person's name, a record
title, or an event type.
