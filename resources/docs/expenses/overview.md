## Overview

Expenses track costs against a property: maintenance, management fees,
taxes, insurance, and so on.

## Viewing expenses

**Expenses** lists every cost recorded, with a running total at the top.

![The Expenses list, showing property, description, category, amount, and date, with a total figure](/docs-screenshots/expenses/expenses-01-index.png)

## Adding an expense

Click **Add expense**.

![The empty Add expense form: Property, Category, Amount, Transaction date, and Description](/docs-screenshots/expenses/expenses-02-create-empty.png)

Select the **Property**, a **Category** (configured in
[Extras](/docs/extras/overview)), the amount, date, and a description.

![The form filled in with a property, category, amount, date, and description](/docs-screenshots/expenses/expenses-03-create-filled.png)

Click **Add expense**.

![The expenses list after adding one, showing a confirmation](/docs-screenshots/expenses/expenses-04-recorded.png)

## Viewing, editing, or removing

The eye icon opens a details panel; the pencil icon opens the same form
pre-filled; the trash icon moves an entry to the trash, reversible via
**Undo** immediately after or from the Trash tab later.
