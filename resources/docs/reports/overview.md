## Overview

Reports are pre-built, downloadable views of your data, organized into four
groups: **Financial** (Income Report, Expense Report, Profit & Loss, Rent
Collection), **Balances** (Rent Arrears, Advance Payments), **Tenants**
(Tenant Roster, New Residents, Expiring Leases), and **Property**
(Vacancies, Deposit Report).

![The Reports page, showing report cards grouped into Financial, Balances, Tenants, and Property columns](/docs-screenshots/reports/reports-01-index.png)

## Running a report

Click any report card. Set a date range and optionally filter by property
or unit; each report has whatever filters make sense for it.

![The Income Report, showing a date range, property/unit filters, a total, and a row-by-row breakdown](/docs-screenshots/reports/reports-02-detail.png)

Click **PDF** or **Excel** to export exactly what's on screen, including
your current filters.
