## Overview

Income covers money coming in that isn't rent — deposit forfeitures, late
fees, and similar one-off receipts against a property. (Rent itself is
tracked through [Payments](/docs/payments/overview).)

## Viewing income

**Income** lists every entry, with a running total at the top.

![The Income list, showing property, description, category, amount, and date, with a total figure](/docs-screenshots/incomes/incomes-01-index.png)

## Adding an income entry

Click **Add income**.

![The empty Add income form: Property, Category, Amount, Transaction date, and Description](/docs-screenshots/incomes/incomes-02-create-empty.png)

Select the **Property**, a **Category** (configured in
[Extras](/docs/extras/overview)), the amount, date, and a description of
what it was for.

![The form filled in with a property, category, amount, date, and description](/docs-screenshots/incomes/incomes-03-create-filled.png)

Click **Add income**.

![The income list after adding one, showing a confirmation](/docs-screenshots/incomes/incomes-04-recorded.png)

## Viewing details

Click the eye icon on any row to open a details panel with the full
breakdown, receipt (if attached), and who recorded it.

![The income details panel, sliding in from the right with the entry's amount, property, category, and metadata](/docs-screenshots/incomes/incomes-05-details-sheet.png)

## Editing or removing

The pencil icon opens the same form pre-filled; the trash icon moves an
entry to the trash, reversible via **Undo** immediately after or from the
Trash tab later.
