## Overview

Properties are the buildings and houses you manage on behalf of a landlord:
either a **Standalone** house (one rentable unit) or a **Multi-Unit**
building (several).

## Viewing properties

**Properties** lists every property, active by default, with a **Trash**
tab for ones you've removed. Search by name, address, or property code, or
use **Filters** to narrow by type or landlord.

![The Properties list, showing property name, type, landlord, and unit/occupancy summary](/docs-screenshots/properties/properties-01-index.png)

## Adding a property

Click **Add property**.

![The empty "Add property" form: Landlord, Type, Name, Address with a map, Description, Amenities, and Photos](/docs-screenshots/properties/properties-02-create-empty.png)

Fill in:

- **Landlord**: a searchable dropdown; type to filter.

  ![The Landlord dropdown filtered to "Ardith", with one matching result highlighted](/docs-screenshots/properties/properties-03-create-landlord-search.png)

- **Type**: Standalone or Multi-Unit.
- **Name**, **Address** (typing searches a real map and drops a pin you can
  fine-tune by dragging), optional **Description**, **Amenities**, and
  **Photos**.

![The form filled in with a landlord, name, address (with the map showing the matched location), and description](/docs-screenshots/properties/properties-04-create-filled.png)

Click **Add property**. You're returned to the properties list with a
confirmation; find your new property there (search for it by name if the
list is long).

A **Standalone** property automatically gets one unit created for it; a
**Multi-Unit** property starts with none; add units yourself, covered in
[Units](/docs/units/overview).

## Viewing a property

Click a property to see its full detail page: photos, occupancy, price
range, location (with a **Get directions** link), and tabs for its
**Units**, **Landlord**, and **Performance**.

![A property's detail page, showing photo, stats, location, and a Units tab listing its one unit](/docs-screenshots/properties/properties-05-show.png)

## Editing a property

Click **Edit property** from the detail page, or the pencil icon from the
list. It's the same form as creating one, pre-filled.

![The edit property form, pre-filled with the property's current details](/docs-screenshots/properties/properties-06-edit.png)

## Removing a property

Click the trash icon on a property's row to move it to the trash. This
doesn't delete anything permanently; a confirmation with an **Undo** option
appears immediately, and the property can still be restored from the
**Trash** tab afterwards.

![The properties list after moving one to trash, showing a confirmation toast with an Undo button](/docs-screenshots/properties/properties-07-trashed.png)

Permanently deleting a property is only possible from the Trash tab, and
asks you to confirm since it can't be undone.
