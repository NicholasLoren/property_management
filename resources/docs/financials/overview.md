## Overview

Financials is a portfolio-wide performance dashboard for a date range you
choose — income, expenses, occupancy, and a per-property breakdown, all in
one place.

## Using it

Set a **From**/**To** date range and optionally narrow to one **Property**.

![The Financials page, showing total income/expenses/net income, occupancy, revenue trend, lease status, and a per-property breakdown table](/docs-screenshots/financials/financials-01-index.png)

The page includes:

- Summary cards: total income, total expenses, net income, occupancy,
  expected monthly rent, and open maintenance requests.
- A **Revenue trend** chart (income vs. expenses over the last 12 months)
  and a **Lease status** breakdown (Draft, Active, Ended, Terminated).
- **Expenses by category** and **Other income by category** charts.
- **Occupancy by property**, highest first.
- A searchable, sortable table of every property's rent collected, other
  income, expenses, net income, occupancy, and open maintenance.

Click **PDF** or **Excel** at any time to export the current view.
